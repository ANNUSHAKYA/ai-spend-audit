# ECONOMICS.md

## What a converted Credex lead is worth

Credex sells discounted AI credits. Assume:
- Average deal size: $500 credit purchase
- Gross margin on resold credits: ~20% = $100 gross profit per sale
- Average customer repurchases 3x per year = $300 gross profit/year per customer
- LTV at 2 years = $600 per converted customer

This is conservative. Enterprise customers buying 
$5,000+ in credits would be worth $3,000+ LTV.

## Conversion funnel math

Assumptions (conservative):
- 1,000 visitors/month to the audit tool
- 40% complete an audit = 400 audits
- 30% of those capture email = 120 leads
- 20% of leads book a Credex consultation = 24 consultations
- 40% of consultations convert to a purchase = ~10 sales/month

At $100 gross profit per sale:
- Month 1 revenue contribution: $1,000
- Month 6 (with growth): $6,000–$10,000/mo

## What makes this $1M ARR in 18 months

Required: ~833 credit purchases per month at $100 GP each,
or fewer larger deals.

Path to get there:
- Month 1–3: 0–50 audits/day, manual outreach, 
  refine conversion
- Month 4–6: 50–200 audits/day via content + word of mouth, 
  10–30 sales/mo
- Month 7–12: Product Hunt, press, partnerships with 
  startup accelerators (YC, Antler) to offer the audit 
  to their portfolio companies
- Month 13–18: Embed the audit tool in Credex onboarding, 
  making it a top-of-funnel for every new signup

Key assumption that must hold: 
audit completion → consultation booking rate stays ≥ 15%. 
If it drops below 10%, the funnel breaks and we'd need 
to either improve the audit quality or change the CTA.
